S1 (synthetic pilot; 2022): 24 novice readers randomly assigned to structured or unstructured abstracts. On a 10-item limitation-identification quiz, structured group mean was 6.0/10 (n=12), unstructured group mean 5.0/10 (n=12). No confidence interval or p-value reported. Single institution; quiz authored by the study team.
S2 (synthetic observational classroom study; 2024): 60 students chose which abstract format to use. Students using structured abstracts reported higher confidence (4.1/5 vs 3.6/5). No objective limitation-identification measure reported. Prior academic performance differed between groups.
S3 (synthetic protocol; 2025): plans a multi-site randomized study with 300 participants, but reports no outcome data.
