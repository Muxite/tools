## Current editorial interpretation

“Minimize whitespace” is translated into **use the canvas purposefully**: reduce large empty regions that carry no meaning by enlarging evidence or adding a helpful visual. Keep margins, hierarchy, and enough space for the slide to breathe. When there is too much content, split it rather than shrinking the type. This wording is an interpretation of your current instruction, not a verbatim recovered preference from another chat.
