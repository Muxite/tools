# Skills to capability capsules

**Problem:** reusable instructions and tools need an executable boundary, dependencies, provenance, and evidence before they can serve as dependable CCs.

The meeting asks to turn “nuaskill” into CCs, add skills continuously, and consider Spec Kit as an AI4Research capability. The exact historical “nuaskill” term remains unresolved; do not silently equate it with a later project. The separately identified [[Designs/Nuwa Briefing/presentation/README|Nuwa briefing]] and [[Designs/Capability Capsule Factory Feature]] now provide a concrete person-to-skill/factory research route. Their proposed AI4R integration remains distinct from a qualified CC.

[[Designs/skills-to-cc-mapping|The conversion design]] frames the first experiment. Start with one skill, declare the work it performs, package only its required resources, and compare its behavior with the original skill and a plain agent. Record human inputs and edits: the optimistic “AI builds AI” expectation is a hypothesis, not the baseline assumption.

Spec Kit may provide a code-development and quality process. Its usefulness inside AI4Research requires a separate integration and evaluation; conversion does not authorize automatic activation or product changes.

Future skill discovery should return attributable candidates and compatibility evidence. Discovery, conversion, evaluation, admission, and activation are distinct steps.
