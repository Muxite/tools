# 2511.02824 · Kosmos: An AI Scientist for Autonomous Discovery

Mitchener, Yiu, Chang, … Rodriques, Hinks, White · Edison Scientific, FutureHouse, Oxford, UCL, WashU, MIT, Stanford … · v2 5 Nov 2025 · 42 pp (26 body)
Read: p1-12 (system, evaluation, discoveries 1-4), limitations (p19)

## Summary
An AI scientist for **data-driven discovery**: given an open-ended objective and a
dataset, it runs up to 12 hours of cycles of parallel data analysis, literature search
and hypothesis generation, then writes 3-4 scientific reports in which every statement
cites a Jupyter notebook or a paper. The innovation is a **structured world model**
shared between agents, which keeps a long run coherent.

## How it works (p2)
- Scientist supplies objective + dataset
- Each cycle: up to **10** parallel tasks for 2 Edison agents, a data-analysis agent
  (Finch lineage) and a literature agent (PaperQA2 lineage); outputs summarised into
  the world model; the world model is queried to propose the next cycle's tasks
- Per run: ~200 rollouts, **42,000 lines of code** over 166 analysis rollouts,
  **1,500 papers** over 36 literature rollouts; 9.8× Robin's code output (p2, Fig. 1b)
- Every report statement links to the notebook or paper it came from

## Results
- **Accuracy of 102 statements from 3 reports**, judged by experts who reproduced or
  looked up each one without seeing Kosmos's evidence: **79.4%** overall; **85.5%**
  data-analysis (n=55), **82.1%** literature (n=28), **57.9%** interpretation/synthesis
  (n=19) (p4, Fig. 1c)
- ~4.1 expert-months of work per run by task count (n=6); collaborators estimate 6.14
  months for a 20-cycle run (n=7); valuable findings scale with cycles up to 20 (p4)
- 7 discoveries across metabolomics, materials, neuroscience, statistical genetics: 3
  reproduce unpublished or preprinted findings not seen at runtime (e.g. nucleotide
  salvage under hypothermia, top-15 fold changes R² = 0.998 vs the original, p6), 4
  are new (e.g. evidence that SOD2 may causally reduce myocardial fibrosis)

## Limitations (stated, p19)
- Accuracy says nothing about whether it chose the most valuable analyses
- "a tendency to invent unorthodox quantitative metrics … conceptually obscure"
- Interpretation only 57% accurate, "likely due to its propensity to conflate
  statistically significant results with scientifically valuable ones"
- "there exists no automated method to reliably evaluate if a claim is accurate, novel,
  and significant"
- ~5 GB dataset ceiling; weak on raw images and sequencing; cannot fetch external
  public data by itself
- Examples of over-reach inside the discoveries: a de novo synthesis conclusion "cannot
  be considered definitive" from steady-state data (p6); an miRNA site annotation that
  does not match target databases (p12)

## Relevance
- ⭐ The 3-way accuracy split is the best external evidence for AI4Research's choice to
  keep claims separate from synthesis and force `unsupported_claims` (REPORT.md §3.5):
  grounded statements score 82-86%, interpretation 58%
- Citation-to-evidence per statement is Tier IV (literature) and Tier II (notebook) on
  the verification ladder, the closest SoTA analogue to AI4Research's verbatim
  grounding, though Kosmos links to evidence without gating on it
- World model = a shared, mutable state re-planned every cycle, the opposite of a
  frozen contract; a clean contrast for §3.2-3.3
