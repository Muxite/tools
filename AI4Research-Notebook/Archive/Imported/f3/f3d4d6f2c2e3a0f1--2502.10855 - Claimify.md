# 2502.10855 · Towards Effective Extraction and Evaluation of Factual Claims (Claimify)

Metropolitansky, Larson · Microsoft Research · v2 6 Jun 2025 (ACL 2025) · 50 pp (9 body)
Read: body p1-8 (framework, method, entailment and coverage results)

## Summary
Proposes (1) a framework for **evaluating claim extraction** for fact-checking, and
(2) **Claimify**, an LLM claim extractor that only extracts a claim when it is confident
of the correct interpretation. Decompose-then-verify fact-checking is only as good as
its claims: a misrepresenting or incomplete claim corrupts the verdict.

## The evaluation framework (p2-4)
- **Entailment**: if the source is true, the claim must be true
- **Coverage**, measured at **element level**: split a sentence into pieces of
  information, label each verifiable or unverifiable; a verifiable element must be
  covered, an unverifiable one must not be explicitly covered
- **Decontextualisation**, measured by outcome rather than judgment: build c_max (the
  claim with all possible context), retrieve evidence for c and c_max, and count it a
  failure only if **the fact-check verdict would change** (7 outcome cases)
- Atomicity is deliberately not a criterion ("lacks a clear endpoint")

## How Claimify works (p4-5)
1. Split answer into sentences (NLTK); build per-stage context windows
2. **Selection**: keep only verifiable content; rewrite mixed sentences; drop "No
   verifiable claims"
3. **Disambiguation**: detect referential and structural ambiguity (incl. author
   interpretation vs fact); if unresolvable from context, label **"Cannot be
   disambiguated" and exclude** (2.4-5.4% of sentences depending on model)
4. **Decomposition** into decontextualised claims; inferred context put in
   **[brackets]** to flag it as less reliable

## Results (gpt-4o, BingCheck: 396 Copilot answers, 6,490 human-labelled sentences)
- Entailment: Claimify 99.0%, VeriScore 99.2%, SAFE 96.6%, DnD 89.1% (Table 1, p6)
- Sentence-level coverage accuracy **91.8%**, macro F1 91.2% (next best AFaCTA 81.6%);
  element-level accuracy **87.9%**, macro F1 83.7% (Table 2, p7)
- Human annotator agreement reached Krippendorff α 0.72 (0.86 on high-confidence) (p5)

## Limitations
- Evaluated on chatbot answers, not scientific papers
- Evaluation prompts are LLM-based (validated on samples of 80)
