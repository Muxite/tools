# Plan · `cc.plan.v1`

**What it is.** The steps that meet the semantic contract, and where each step's inputs come from. Each step states what it needs and which capsules were proposed for it. The Plan does not bind capsules; freeze does.

**Rules that matter most here:** INV-4 (planner settings live in the policy), INV-5 (no copied scores).

## What: the core

Extends [`cc.common.v1`](common.md), with `scope.run_id`. The envelope's `producer.component` is the planner-binder, and `producer.version` names the planner used (e.g. `pass_through@1`).

## How: lives elsewhere

| Was here | Now lives in |
|---|---|
| `planner {kind, version}` | the envelope's `producer`; the planner's settings in policy `planner` |
| `symphony {goal_score, edge_confidence}` | `ext.symphony`, optional. Symphony's public `plan()` does not return them, and `goal_score` is a seed count |
| `over.max_parallel` | policy `dispatch` |
| when to require `certified` | policy `levels` |

## Extension points

- A new planner is a new `producer.version` value and a policy section; the Plan does not change.
- `ext.<planner>` for the planner's own evidence (scores, reasons). No gate reads it.
- `requires` may gain optional keys (after M1: `restrict_to`, `judge_tolerance`).
