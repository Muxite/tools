## Human synopsis

Investigate small, source-linked context rather than assuming a larger library helps. Procedures, source references, observed trajectories and derived lessons have different jobs. A relevant candidate can still have incompatible inputs, unmet preconditions or unacceptable effects; discovering it does not establish eligibility.

Keep assessment and authority separate. A verifier supplies a verdict; the protected host gate checks its subject/evidence binding and applies frozen policy to control advancement. Separate invocation does not establish statistical independence or truth. Likewise, a remembered lesson is advisory: relevance or recency alone cannot resolve every conflict.

The important evidence gaps are transfer to scientific workflows, contract-aware capsule selection, and reliable reuse after protocol/environment changes. The reviewed benchmarks largely cover bounded facts, tools or generated-file tasks. Formal composition work is an analogy, not an agent-system validation.

## Five useful experiments to consider

| Question | Small comparison |
|---|---|
| Does the consumer benefit from selected context? | No feed vs curated procedure vs experience; include irrelevant/near-neighbor controls and inspect regressions, completion and cost. |
| Does verification stop convincing wrong output? | Good/wrong/unsupported/injected subjects with independently adjudicated outcomes; record false advancement and blocking. Never score a verifier with its own verdict. |
| Can lessons be safely reused later? | Current/stale/conflicting/unresolved lessons, including a clean session after compaction; inspect source retention and actual effects. |
| Can research be repeated and reconstructed? | Frozen protocol/environment, repeated runs and interruptions, including valid negative outcomes; distinguish science variation from infrastructure defects. |
| Does the sidecar improve diagnosis? | Ordinary logs vs bounded registered tools on the same owned failure; compare diagnosis accuracy/time and confinement. |

These are proposals, not additional gates. Cases, thresholds and implementation require an assigned experiment. Preserve actual context, environment and evidence; disclose confounds and avoid best-run-only claims.
