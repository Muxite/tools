# 2411.04468 · Magentic-One: A Generalist Multi-Agent System for Solving Complex Tasks

Fourney, Bansal, Mozannar, Tan, et al. · Microsoft Research (AutoGen) · arXiv 7 Nov 2024 · 31 pp (20 body)
Read: p1-6 (design, orchestrator ledgers, evaluation setup); results from contributions

## Summary
A generalist team of 5 agents on AutoGen: **Orchestrator**, Coder, Computer Terminal,
File Surfer, Web Surfer. The Orchestrator plans, keeps structured working memory,
assigns subtasks, detects stalls and **re-plans** to recover. Also releases
**AutoGenBench** for repeatable, isolated agent evaluation.

## How it works (p5)
- **Outer loop, Task Ledger**: given or verified facts, facts to look up, facts to derive,
  educated guesses, and the plan; created at the start and **rewritten when progress
  stalls**
- **Inner loop, Progress Ledger**: is the task complete? are we in an unproductive loop?
  is progress being made? who speaks next, and with what instruction? A **stall count
  > 2** sends control back to the outer loop to update facts and re-plan
- Agents can be added or removed without retuning prompts (p2)
- AutoGenBench: repetition and isolation so side-effecting agents do not interfere
  across runs (p2-3)

## Results (p3)
GAIA **38%** task completion, WebArena **32.8%**, AssistantBench **27.7%** accuracy;
statistically competitive with SoTA including benchmark-specialised systems, without
changing the agents between benchmarks.

## Relevance
- REPORT.md §3.4: the canonical **model-led, re-planning orchestrator**. The task ledger
  is a mutable plan owned by the LLM, which is the design AI4Research's freeze rules out
  after planning
- The "facts / guesses / to-derive" ledger is a useful comparison for AI4Research's
  IntentIR fields (constraints, ambiguities, unknowns), which are fixed at compile time
  rather than rewritten on stall
- AutoGenBench's isolation-for-side-effects is the evaluation-side counterpart of
  AI4Research's sandboxed experiment scopes
