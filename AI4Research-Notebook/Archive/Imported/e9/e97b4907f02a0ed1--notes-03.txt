SAY: AI4Research takes a research request written in plain language and turns it into checked evidence and a report. 7 terms carry the talk. A capsule describes 1 capability in a YAML file: what it takes in, what it produces and what it may touch; 79 exist and every one was written by hand, which is what the next talk is about. The plan is a graph of steps, and a node is 1 step with 1 capsule bound to it. An operator is what actually does the step: a model call, a coding agent or a tool. A gate decides pass or fail for 1 node's output. A sprint is 1 request from start to finish. And the freeze is the moment the plan, its capsules and its grading rules are hashed into 1 contract, after which nothing in the plan can change.
