# Intent context

Updated October 7, 2026. Working context, not a parallel architecture specification.

The design is the final compact whole-system layer for human review. Review breadth first: module purposes, connections, readiness and future seams. Deepen only at consequential boundaries; agents expand schemas, implementation and concrete verification. The PRD always accompanies the design and retains product requirements.

The full and very condensed designs have the same M1 scope. The condensed projection intentionally drops details. The current reduced-input experiment builds only Intent Compilation and Verification Slice; it does not demonstrate complete M1 adequacy.

Quality should be judged through use: inspect actual output and the intended consumer's use, retain observed failures and limit conclusions to demonstrated cases. Mechanical conformance alone does not establish fidelity or usability. Optional polish need not block after mandatory obligations pass.

Keep future ideas and extra rationale here until a design decision needs them. Broader planning, reusable/composite capabilities, richer model routes, wider RSI targets and lifecycle extensions must preserve existing authority and evidence seams; these ideas do not authorize extra M1 work.
