SAY: Kosmos is the scale point in this comparison. A scientist gives it an objective and a dataset, and each cycle launches up to 10 parallel tasks across a data-analysis agent and a literature-search agent, whose outputs go into a shared world model that proposes the next cycle. 1 run is over 200 rollouts, 42,000 lines of code and 1,500 papers. Every statement in the report cites a notebook or a publication. Experts blind to both rated 79.4% of 102 statements accurate, and only 57.9% of the synthesis ones. So what for us: that accuracy was checked after the run, and 4 in 10 synthesis statements were wrong. AI4Research gates each claim before it reaches the report and keeps claims apart from synthesis, which is where Kosmos was weakest.
