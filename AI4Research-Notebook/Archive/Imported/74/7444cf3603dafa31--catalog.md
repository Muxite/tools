Runtime records also require `schema_version`, `id`, `scope`, `at`, `producer`. Producers are authorized roles, not arbitrary users supplying the matching JSON shape. All required schemas resolve through [catalog.json](catalog.json); the shared primitives are [common.schema.json](schemas/common.schema.json). The [example index](examples/index.json) includes additional examples for Intent, Requirements and RSI.
