# 2601.13217 · Beyond Single-shot Writing: Deep Research Agents are Unreliable at Multi-turn Report Revision (MR DRE)

Chen, Li, Nie, Zhang, Ye, Zhao · NYU, NYU Shanghai, Alberta, Princeton, Waterloo, Verdent AI · arXiv 19 Jan 2026 (ACL 2026) · 42 pp (11 body)
Read: p1-7 (protocol, feedback simulation, metrics, first results table)

## Summary
Existing deep-research benchmarks score a single report. MR DRE adds **multi-turn
revision**: the agent revises its report after user feedback, and the suite measures
both whether the feedback was incorporated and **what else broke**. Across 5 deep
research agents, agents address >90% of requested edits but damage **16-27% of content
they had already covered**, and citation quality drops.

## How it works (p3-6)
- Questions and expert checklists from ResearchRubrics, RigorousBench, ResearcherBench
- **Comprehensiveness**: weighted checklist coverage, ternary LLM grading (0/0.5/1)
- **Factuality** (adapted VeriScore): LLM extracts atomic claims; judge checks each
  against crawled cited pages → **citation faithfulness** (supported / cited claims) and
  **claim groundedness** (supported / all claims)
- **Presentation**: 10 binary checklist items
- Feedback simulation: content (from unmet checklist items), format (21 seed requests),
  self-reflection ("Please reflect on your current report and revise it"); human-validated
- Metrics: **Incorporation Rate** and **Break Rate** (share of previously covered criteria
  whose score drops)

## Results
- Turn 1 baselines for OpenAI Deep Research (Table, p7): citation faithfulness 63.6 /
  63.5 / 80.3 and groundedness 28.8 / 33.5 / 41.4 on the 3 datasets
- Self-reflection alone often **lowers** scores (e.g. OpenAI DR faithfulness −10.3 on
  ResearchRubrics after one reflect turn)
- Break rate 16-27%; over more turns some agents make "minimal to negative progress";
  prompt engineering and a dedicated reviser sub-agent do not fix it (p1-2)

## Limitations
LLM judges throughout; simulated (not real) users, though human-validated.

## Relevance
- REPORT.md §3.2: direct evidence for **freezing what has been accepted**. Unconstrained
  revision regresses prior, already-correct content; AI4Research's frozen plan and
  append-only ledger are designed so accepted work cannot be silently rewritten
- The groundedness numbers (29-41% of all claims supported) are a sobering baseline for
  deep-research reports and worth one line in §3.5
