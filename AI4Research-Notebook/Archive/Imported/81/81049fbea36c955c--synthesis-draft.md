# Research closeout synthesis (draft)

**Optional research only.** Papers do not add M1 requirements or validate this architecture.

1. **Assessment is fallible; authority is separate.** Judge studies show position/prompt sensitivity, adversarial failures, and correlated errors on particular benchmarks. They motivate challenge fixtures, but do not estimate JiuwenSwarm error rates or make separate calls statistically independent. A protected gate is a design boundary, not a paper result; it must bind evidence to the run and apply policy outside the verifier.

2. **Retrieval relevance is not eligibility.** Tool-use studies separate discovery, argument grounding, state and abstention failures; AutoLabs’ three-procedure RAG ablation helps one configuration and hurts another. None studies typed capsule selection. Hard filtering on pins, preconditions, types and effects remains a proposed experiment, not validated best practice.

3. **Scientific evidence has levels.** Coscientist reports GC-MS product evidence after documentation-based tool recovery, with manual plate movement; its optimization queried mapped lookup data. AutoLabs physically ran 2/5 cases. RoboCulture reports one 15-hour yeast run with manual setup/intervention and a parallel plate-reader comparison. Import/simulation do not prove scientific outcomes or broad reproducibility.
