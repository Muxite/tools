# Human plan: CC architecture and M1 coordination

**Planning date:** October 14, 2026. **October 30 outcome:** publish an indexed M1 evidence set and demonstrate the artifact-dataset baseline with its limits. The current main path uses generic Leader/SwarmFlow; it is not an integrated AI4Research Planner. The one-shot trial uses four fixed roles and worker-data replay, not interactive conversation. This is a coordination plan, not a coding-task register. Product requirements remain owned by their authors; use the maintained design and supplied PRD for implementation authority.

**Nov 30 gate:** review the M1 baseline evidence, November feature records and December readiness. The gate judges recorded outcomes, not task labels. Missing prerequisites keep the affected decision open.

## Capacity and scheduling rules

- Dates are work windows, not proof that every parallel label consumes a full day. Reuse shared outputs and agent-produced drafts; record human review effort once.
- Keep each implementation, investigation or review subtask within three working days. Split longer work at an acceptance boundary; use role labels until availability is confirmed.
- Investigation notes are subtasks linked to the evidence-producing work, not standalone resource rows that duplicate effort.
- If required code, access, cases or interfaces are missing, hold the dependent gate, reduce optional scope or move the dates. Do not claim unavailable evidence as a pass.
- The October benchmark sidecar remains development tooling and does not become a product startup, test, or release prerequisite.
