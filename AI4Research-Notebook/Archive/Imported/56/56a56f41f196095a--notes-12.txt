SAY: Claims are picked out of paper text by a word rule, words like 'improves' or 'found', not by a model. Each claim is then judged by this table, top to bottom. Take a real test case: 'Reranker A improves ranking quality over reranker B.' The word 'improves' matches, the sentence and its section are hashed, and there's no experiment, so the table stops at 'partially supported', 0.85. That verdict certifies that the sentence is in its cited section, so a wrong or retracted paper passes. That's provenance, not truth. A claim judged insufficient still becomes a conclusion in the report, so the list of unsupported claims stays empty. And a claim the word rule misses never appears anywhere, so nothing shows it was missed.
IF ASKED:
