# 2604.25256 · AutoResearchBench: Benchmarking AI Agents on Complex Scientific Literature Discovery

AutoResearchBench Team · arXiv v1 Apr 2026 · 28 pp (about 10 body)
Read: body p1-10 this session

## Summary
Isolates the **literature-finding** step of autonomous research, the step every claim
verifier depends on. 1,000 human-verified queries over a 3M-paper arXiv corpus, split
into Deep Research (find the one paper satisfying fused full-text clues, or prove none
exists) and Wide Research (enumerate the complete set). Frontier models that exceed 80%
on BrowseComp score **under 10%** here.

## How it works (p3-6)
- Deep Research: 600 queries, |Y*| ∈ {0,1}, 90% single-answer and 10% deliberately
  unsatisfiable to test abstention. Clues are mined from appendices, proofs, ablations
  and affiliations — never dataset names or headline claims — then fuzzified at topic
  and detail level and pruned to **minimal sufficiency** (p4-5)
- Wide Research: 400 queries, 3,692 answers, mean 9.23 per query, range [2,34]
  (Table 1, p6). Answer sets are built from an entity graph, expanded iteratively, and
  admitted only on unanimous consent of three LLMs plus a human audit (p5)
- Metrics: exact-match Accuracy for Deep (any partial credit is zero), **IoU** for Wide,
  which punishes both missed papers and out-of-scope inclusions (p6)
- All models run in one ReAct agent over the same DeepXiv search tool, so differences
  reflect model rather than framework (p7)

## Results
- Best scores (Table 2, p8): Claude-Opus-4.6 **9.39%** Deep accuracy;
  Gemini-3.1-Pro-Preview **9.31%** Wide IoU. Most models, open and closed, are below 5%
- Opus-4.6 averages 28.1 turns on Deep and still **fails over 90% of cases** (p8)
- More effort does not help: GPT-5.4 gets 7.44% Deep in 6.1 turns while Deepseek-V3.2
  (28.8 turns) and Kimi-K2.5 (27.0 turns) get 4.21% and 4.69% (p7-8)
- Opposite Wide failure modes: Opus-4.6 expands over 27.11 turns and fails to filter
  (IoU 6.56%), while Seed-2.0-Pro and GPT-5.4 stop after 4.15 and 3.69 turns and never
  reach coverage (p8)
- IoU distribution for Gemini-3.1-pro over 100 cases: 31.0% score exactly 0, 66.0% score
  below 0.1, and **nothing** reaches 0.4 (Fig. 4, p7)
- End-to-end products do worse than the ReAct harness on Wide: Alphaxiv 4.31, GPT Deep
  Research 4.06, AI-studio Gemini-3.1-Pro 4.84 IoU (Table 2, p8)
- Tool matters: averaged over four matched models, Deep accuracy drops from 5.42% on the
  DeepXiv tool to a lower figure on an open-web (jina) backend (p8, Table 3 p9)

## Limitations (stated / observed)
- ⚠ Computer science only, eight CS subdomains (Fig. 3, p6); no natural-science
  literature, which is where most AI4Research use cases sit
- ⚠ End-to-end systems are evaluated on a 50-query sample, not the full set, so their
  Deep column is reported as "11/50" rather than a comparable percentage (p7, Table 2)
- Wide ground truth is an approximation of completeness built by LLM consensus plus
  human audit; absolute IoU is therefore an upper-bounded quantity

## Relevance
- **Report §3.4-3.5 (claims and citations)**: the single hardest external datum for the
  claim that an agent can verify a claim against the literature. If retrieval of the
  right paper succeeds under 10% of the time, citation-accuracy scores on benchmarks
  like DeepResearch Bench measure formatting fidelity more than evidential grounding
- The abstention subset (10% unsatisfiable Deep queries) is directly relevant to
  AI4Research's verification framing: a verifier's most valuable output is often
  "no supporting paper exists", and this is the only benchmark here that scores it
- Cheap to run, one ReAct loop and one search tool, but scores will be near the floor;
  useful as a diagnostic, poor as a first headline number
