# 2606.15497 · Towards End-to-End Automation of AI Research (The AI Scientist, Nature 2026)

Yamada, Lange, Lu, Lu, Hu, Foerster, Ha, Clune · Sakana AI, Oxford FLAIR, UBC, Vector · arXiv 31 Mar 2026 (Nature s41586-026-10265-5) · 182 pp (7 main + methods + supplement)
Read: main text and methods (p1-12); supplement sampled for prompts (p35-41) and failure analysis (p77)

## Summary
The journal version of The AI Scientist (v1 2024, v2 2025). A system that generates
ideas, writes code, runs experiments, plots, writes the manuscript and reviews it,
restricted to machine-learning research done entirely on computer. Headline: one fully
AI-generated paper passed first-round peer review at an ICLR 2025 workshop.

## How it works (p2-3, Fig. 1)
1. **Ideation**: grows an archive of research directions with title, rationale and
   experimental plan; filters novelty against Semantic Scholar and the web, discarding
   ideas "too similar to existing literature"
2. **Experimentation**, 2 modes:
   - template-based: extends a human-provided code template linearly
   - template-free: writes its own code, then **agentic tree search** in 4 stages
     (initial investigation, hyperparameter tuning, research agenda, ablations); the
     best checkpoint of each stage seeds the next. Journal-style notes after each run
3. **Write-up**: fills a LaTeX template section by section; citations found by querying
   Semantic Scholar over 20 rounds with a justification per citation
4. **Automated Reviewer**: ensemble of 5 NeurIPS-guideline reviews + an Area-Chair
   meta-review (p3)

## Results
- **Automated Reviewer vs humans** (Table 1, p4): balanced accuracy 0.69 ± 0.04 on
  2017-2024 papers and 0.66 ± 0.03 on 2025 papers (after cutoff), vs 0.66 for human
  NeurIPS 2021 reviewers; F1 0.62/0.67 vs 0.49; but FPR 0.45/0.52 vs human 0.17
- Paper quality (as judged by the Automated Reviewer) rises with model release date
  (p < 0.00001, Fig. 1B) and with tree-search compute (Fig. 2C)
- **Workshop test** (p4-6): 3 manuscripts submitted blind to ICLR 2025 ICBINB among 43
  papers; 1 scored **6, 7, 6 = 6.33**, above the average acceptance threshold, top 45%.
  It reported a negative result. Withdrawn by pre-set protocol. Internal human review:
  meets workshop bar, none meets main-conference bar

## Limitations (stated, p5-7)
- **Humans filtered outputs at each stage**: "We manually filtered the most promising
  outputs at each stage" and chose 3 submissions on topic fit, whether the code
  "correctly implemented the proposed idea and ran without errors", and formatting (p5)
- Workshop acceptance 70% vs 32% main track; 1 of 3 accepted; "does not yet meet the
  standards for top-tier publications, nor even consistently for workshops" (p6)
- Failure modes: naive ideas, incorrect implementations, weak rigour, experimental
  errors, duplicated figures, "many types of hallucinations such as inaccurate
  citations" (p6-7); supplement example: claimed V100 GPUs when H100s were used (p77)
- Computational experiments only

## Critical notes
- Hallucination control is **prompt text**: "do NOT hallucinate data" (p35), "Do not
  hallucinate data. Data must either be loaded from .npy files or copied from the JSON
  summaries" (p37), "Do not hallucinate new citations or any results not in the logs"
  (p39). Nothing checks these mechanically
- The quality trend (Fig. 1B, 2C) is measured **by the system's own LLM reviewer**
  (ladder Tier VIII), whose false-positive rate is ~3× the human rate
- Verification-gap survey (2608.05179) codes v1 as L4 with LLM review, code, prompts,
  seeds and traces released (Table 3, p11)

## Relevance
- The reference end-to-end system for REPORT.md §3.5 and §4
- Sharpest contrast with AI4Research: data provenance required by prompt vs checked in
  code (verbatim-quote test, verdict gate); grading by the system's own reviewer vs
  frozen deterministic evaluators. Fair note: the AI Scientist does execute every
  experiment node, so its numbers do come from runs
- The human filtering step is a hidden-labour point (survey Table 9) worth naming when
  comparing autonomy
