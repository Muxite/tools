SAY: These 2 results measure different tools. Alita-G scored final answers on the same GAIA questions its tools came from. Beyond Task Completion replayed 222 tools from 3 other protocols on held-out tests of their own job, and 215 scored zero. A task checks only the call it needed, inside the regime the tool was fitted to, so an overfit tool passes there and fails its capability's full contract. Alita-G's gain is 13 questions of 165 turning right and none turning wrong, and its tools were never replayed. Harvesting from 1 half of GAIA and scoring both halves would settle it.
