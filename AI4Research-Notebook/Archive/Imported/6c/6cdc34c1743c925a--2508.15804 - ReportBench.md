# 2508.15804 · ReportBench: Evaluating Deep Research Agents via Academic Survey Tasks

Li, Zeng, Cheng, Ma, Jia · ByteDance BandAI · arXiv Aug 2025 · 18 pp (10 body)
Read: body p1-9 (construction, evaluation pipeline, Table 1)

## Summary
A benchmark that turns **published, peer-reviewed arXiv survey papers** into research
prompts, so each task has an expert-built ground truth: the survey's own reference
list. It scores deep-research reports on (1) whether they cite the right literature and
(2) whether their statements are faithful, both cited and uncited.

## How it works (p2-6)
- 678 peer-reviewed surveys (post-2020, "survey/review" + "published/accepted",
  GPT-4o filter), references parsed from LaTeX; 100 sampled across 10 domains
- **Reverse prompt engineering**: an LLM writes a query whose ideal answer is the survey,
  at sentence, paragraph or detail-rich level, with a date cutoff; models were caught
  retrieving the original survey anyway, so prompts also forbid citing it (p4)
- **Cited statements**: extract statement-URL pairs → scrape page → LLM finds the most
  relevant passage → LLM judges consistency (**match rate**); intermediate outputs kept
  for human inspection
- **Uncited statements**: 2 web-connected models × 3 judgments each, majority vote
  (**factual accuracy**)

## Results (Table 1, p7)
| System | Ref. precision | Ref. recall | Cited match rate | Uncited factual acc. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OpenAI Deep Research | 0.385 | 0.033 | 78.87% | 95.83% |
| Gemini Deep Research | 0.145 | 0.036 | 72.94% | 92.21% |
| claude4-sonnet (+search) | 0.337 | 0.021 | 73.67% | 92.64% |
| openai-o3 (+search) | 0.299 | 0.031 | 31.43% | 82.22% |

- ⭐ **Recall against the survey's references is 1-4%** for every system: agents find a
  tiny fraction of the literature an expert survey actually cites
- Roughly 1 in 4 cited statements is not supported by its own citation even for the
  best agents

## Limitations
- Assumes the survey's reference list is the right answer, which penalises valid
  alternative citations
- Every faithfulness judgment is an LLM (gpt-4o, Gemini) (Tier IV-by-LLM / VIII)
- Commercial outputs collected manually in a 2-week window (p6)

## Relevance
- REPORT.md §3.5 and §3.7: the "cited but unsupported" rate (21-27% for deep-research
  agents) is the failure AI4Research's literature path avoids by only giving a verdict to
  a claim whose text occurs in its cited section (a substring check), at the cost of
  only ever quoting verbatim
- The 1-4% recall is the strongest argument that literature *coverage*, not just
  citation accuracy, is unsolved; AI4Research's discovery stage has the same exposure
  (see its study-protocol `unresolved_fields`)
