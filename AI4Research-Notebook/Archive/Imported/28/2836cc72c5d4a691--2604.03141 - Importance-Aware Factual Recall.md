# 2604.03141 · Beyond Precision: Importance-Aware Recall for Factuality Evaluation in Long-Form LLM Generation

arXiv v1 April 2026 · 13 pp (8 body) · "Preprint. Under review."
Read: body p2-p9 including Tables 1-2 and Figures 1-2.

## Summary
Argues that the whole FActScore/VeriScore/SAFE family measures only **precision** — are
the emitted claims supported — and never **recall** — are the facts that should have been
there present. It builds a retrieval-conditioned reference fact set per query, weights each
reference fact by relevance and salience, and reports precision, recall and F1 together.

## How it works (p3-p5)
- Reference set: retrieve top-k documents → VeriScore-style LLM atomic fact extraction at
  chunk level → agglomerative clustering to drop near-duplicates → F(q)
- **Importance score** imp(f;q) = α·relevance + β·salience, each rated 1-5 by an LLM judge
  and normalised; facts ranked, top-K* kept as the "should-include" set (p4)
- Claims from the model output extracted with the same VeriScore procedure
- Precision = fraction of claims labelled SUPPORTED against the retrieved evidence;
  separately reports CONTRADICTED and NOT-SUPPORTED rates (p4)
- Recall = fraction of reference facts labelled COVERED; importance-weighted variant (p5)
- ρ = |claims| / |facts| used to separate "recall by verbosity" from "claim efficiency" (p7)

## Results
- Datasets: FactScore-Bio (183 prompts), LongFact (200 sampled of 2,280), LongForm (200)
- ⭐ **Precision/recall/F1 are low everywhere** (p6 Table 1): Bio best is
  Gemini-2.5-flash-lite at 14.1 / 10.9 / 9.4; LongFact best is GPT-4o-mini at
  38.6 / 42.6 / 36.8; LongForm best F1 is Llama-3.1-70B at 27.7 / 8.5 / 8.9
- ⭐ **"Not supported" is the single largest verdict category across every model and
  dataset** (p7, Fig 1); outright contradictions are a smaller share and peak on Bio
- Recall is consistently worse than precision → "factual incompleteness remains a major
  limitation" (p1, p9)
- Verbosity buys recall at the cost of precision, but only for the most verbose model
  (Gemini-2.5-flash-lite, ρ up to 8.1 on LongFact); GPT-4o-mini reaches near-max recall at
  much lower ρ (p7 Table 2)
- Importance weighting: at K = 1, salience-only ranking is comparable or better than the
  combined score; at K = |F*|, the combined score beats salience-only, i.e. **models cover
  the few most important facts well and the full relevant set poorly** (p8)

## Limitations
⚠ **Every stage is an LLM** — retrieval-fact extraction, claim decomposition, importance
rating, coverage judging, support judging — with **no human validation of any label**
anywhere in the paper. The authors acknowledge error propagation but measure none (p9).
⚠ The precision numbers are implausible as absolute factuality rates (6-14% on Bio, where
FActScore-family work reports far higher); they are a property of a narrow
retrieval-conditioned reference set, so **they are comparable within this paper only** and
must not be quoted as "LLMs are 10% factual".
⚠ Models are a generation behind (Llama-3.1, Qwen2.5, Mistral-7B, GPT-4o-mini,
Gemini-2.5-flash-lite); no frontier 2026 model is evaluated.
⚠ α, β are set to 1 with no sensitivity analysis; K* is a free parameter.

## Relevance
- Useful for one thing only, but it is the right thing: it names **claim-extraction recall
  as a first-class missing metric** and shows that the entire precision-only literature
  (FActScore, VeriScore, SAFE, and by extension any verdict table that only judges the
  claims that were extracted) cannot see omission at all
- ⚠ Direct caution for AI4Research: a deterministic regex extractor is a *precision*
  device. It can certify that every sentence it pulled is in the cited section; it cannot
  by construction detect a claim the report should have made and did not, nor a claim the
  regex failed to pull. The report should state that its recall is unmeasured
- The finding that "not supported" dominates "contradicted" matches DeepFact's
  27% natural-unsupported rate: the dominant failure is **absence of support, not
  falsehood** — which is precisely what a provenance check cannot distinguish
