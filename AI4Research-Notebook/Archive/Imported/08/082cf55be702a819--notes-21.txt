SAY: The manifest is written first, because the tests need something to check against. Research steps often have no right answer, so the tests check what the capsule promises, not a research result. Each field is a promise, the gate checks it with a fixed rule, and a broken promise has its own rejection code. The most important is effects: what the capsule touched in the sandbox log must stay inside what it declared, and none of the systems in these papers checks that. The protected box is due to the DGM lesson, enforced by write access, not by hiding.
IF ASKED:

- Where quality needs judgment, a different model from the builder grades it, and its verdict enters only as a recorded result
- The duplicate check compares a candidate with every registered capsule except the one it versions
