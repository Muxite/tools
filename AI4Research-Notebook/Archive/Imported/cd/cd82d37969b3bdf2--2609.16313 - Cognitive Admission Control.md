## Summary
An agent may be **authorised** to mutate infrastructure (e.g. promote a database replica)
while lacking **evidence the mutation is ready** (replica current, old primary fenced).
CAC is a policy-governed admission layer between an agent's proposed tool call and the
execution gateway. A policy maps a **typed proposal plus a risk vector** to **assurance
obligations**; a deterministic evaluator discharges each as satisfied / violated /
unknown; missing evidence yields a **remediation request**, a violation yields denial, and
success yields a **signed, single-use, expiring certificate** that the gateway checks
before dispatch (p1, p4-5). The authors stress the guarantees are **policy-relative**:
they do not make the policy correct, the evidence true, or the world safe (p1, p6, p12).
Evaluation is a TypeScript prototype on **13 constructed failover state machines, no
language model invoked** (p8-9).

## How it works (p2-8)
- **Proposal** q = ⟨intent, tool operation, params, scope, principal, state version,
  constraints⟩ (Def. 1, p3)
- **Risk vector** ρ = ⟨severity, blast radius, irreversibility, observational
  uncertainty, dependency exposure, adverse plausibility⟩, a product preorder of policy
  categories, "not calibrated probabilities" (p3)
- **Evidence receipt** binds claim, class, source, provenance, scope, observation time,
  state version, integrity, dependencies (Def. 4, p3). "Authenticity ... does not
  establish the truth of the claim" (p3)
- **Obligation** ω: predicate, verification kind, scope, accepted evidence classes,
  freshness horizon Δt_max, set constraints (witness sets: distinct sources, principal
  separation), optional structural-cut (EFD) requirement, guard templates, and mode
  required or advisory (Def. 5, p3). Example JSON in Listing 1 (p15)
- **Discharge** (Eq. 12, p4): SATISFIED if only positive maximal witnesses,
  VIOLATED if only negative, UNKNOWN(Conflict) if both, UNKNOWN(c) if neither, with c a
  reproducible diagnostic (stale, untrusted, wrong version, missing). The verdict and
  canonical witness choice are deterministic for fixed inputs; certificates are not
  byte-identical (fresh nonce, issue time) (p2)
- **Bounded search**: the prototype enumerates subsets up to **12** eligible receipts per
  obligation, else returns unknown; truncated search never permits (p4)
- **Five outcomes** (p5, Alg. 1 p8): PERMIT(certificate), DENY, DEFER (unresolved
  obligations plus typed acquisition requests), ESCALATE (unresolved approval
  obligations), ABORT (budget exhausted)
- **Certificate** binds subject, authority context, mode, proposal digest, envelope,
  policy epoch, witness-manifest digest, guards, issue/expiry, nonce, signature (p5).
  Lifetime ≤ earliest receipt expiry (Eq. 16-17). Gateway checks 9 conjuncts (Eq. 18)
  then an atomic nonce CAS: **at-most-once dispatch, not exactly-once effects** (p5-7)
- **Formal results** (p6-7): authority-readiness separation (Thm 1), budget does not
  determine admission (Thm 2), compensatory scores can bypass a required condition
  (Prop. 1), admission refinement for Alg. 1 (Thm 3), threshold monotonicity (Prop. 2),
  structural-cut bound imported from EFD (Prop. 3). All conditional; "the prototype is not
  a verified implementation of the entire specification" (p6)

## Results
- **Trials**: 13 scenarios × 7 controllers × 30 seeds = **2,730** (p9). Seeds vary
  identifiers and lag magnitude; they "do not sample independent operational incidents"
  (p9). CAC itself runs **390** of them
- ⭐ **Table 2 (p10)**, attempts / complete / harm over 390 trials each: AuthOnly
  390 / 180 / **180**; LivePolicy 180 / 120 / **30**; **CAC 150 / 120 / 0**; NoGuard
  180 / 120 / 30; NoRemediation 120 / **90** / 0; NoTypedEvidence 180 / 120 / 30;
  NoEFD 180 / 120 / 30
- **LivePolicy matches CAC's 120 completions**; its only extra harm is the 30
  correlated-witness trials. "This suite therefore does not establish a general utility
  advantage over a competent live-policy check" (p10)
- Mechanism contrasts, 30 trials each (p10): guards block the pre-gate drift; typed
  evidence classes refuse a wrong-class "zero lag" receipt; structural cut (cut 1 < 2)
  blocks three correlated approvers; remediation turns 30 underprepared intents into
  completions (NoRemediation 90 vs 120). Evidence that stays unavailable blocks both
  variants in all 30 trials, though the target was safe
- **Cost (Table 3, p11)**: full local path median 4.65-5.69 ms (1 obligation), 24.91-26.86
  ms (8); p99 up to 58.39 ms. Full discharge for 12 receipts 67.31-72.69 ms. **9,000**
  timing samples (3 processes × 1,000 × 3 workloads) (p11)
- 133 passing tests; finite cross-check of 43,561 three-voter profile/rule combinations
  (p10, p14-15)
- No confidence intervals or significance claims, by design (p10)

## Limitations
- Stated (p12): policy adequacy is not inferred; signatures do not prove sensor truth;
  undisclosed common causes break the cut bound; conjunctive policies can block
  indefinitely; targets are local models with no replication, fencing, partitions or
  recovery; no production reliability, model-family, or cost claims
- Stated (p13): **deterministic admission, obligations and repair are not claimed as
  novel** (Mnemosyne already has them); no implementation comparison with it
- Stated (p10): a **previous 5,261-record batch** had controller-dependent labels,
  scripted model comparators and incomplete timers; it is kept but "not evidence". The
  reported study is a corrected rerun
- ⚠ Despite "Cognitive" in the title, no language model is run: receipts are synthetic
  and the scenarios were "constructed around CAC's mechanisms" (p8-9)
- ⚠ The 2,730 figure counts all seven controllers; the zero-harm result is 390 CAC trials
  over 13 designed fixtures, 30 seeds each
- ⚠ Envelope (non-exact) certificates: general semantic coverage is outside the
  implemented fragment; the normal path mints exact-action certificates (p8)

## Relevance
- **Supports** the gate's *shape*: typed obligations declared up front, a deterministic
  ternary verdict where "missing evidence" is distinct from "false", a request for the
  missing evidence instead of a silent reject, and a signed record binding the admitted
  item's digest to the evidence manifest (p4-5). That is a reasonable template for a
  capsule admission verdict record
- **Supports** conjunctive, non-compensatory admission over a scalar score (Prop. 1, p6)
- **Does not support** anything about capability packages, libraries, versions or
  append-only storage. What CAC admits is a **single dispatch of one consequential
  infrastructure action**; its certificate is short-lived (bounded by evidence freshness)
  and single-use (p5). A library admission is persistent, the opposite lifetime
- **Does not supply evidence** for such a gate: the results are 13 constructed failover
  fixtures with no LLM, and CAC ties the live-policy baseline on completion (p10)
- Verdict on the analogy: **fair as a pattern citation for the evaluator interface,
  a stretch as prior art for admitting versioned packages into a library**
