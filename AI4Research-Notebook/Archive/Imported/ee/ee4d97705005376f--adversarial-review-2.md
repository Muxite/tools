# Adversarial review 2: Quiet-window profiling deck

Reviewer stance: hostile but fair. Formed independently. I did not read adversarial-review.md, deck_v1.json, build_spec_v1.py or the v1 pptx.
Inputs read: deck.json (30 slides), rendered slides 2, 3, 5 to 9, 12 to 16, notes 12, 13, 22, report.md, chart-data.json, planner_baselines.py (I ran it), gb10-profiling-plan.md, literature/README.md, and the design-package files named in the brief plus field-catalog.md, owner-rulings.md, model-catalog.md and model-routing.md where the deck's claims led there.

Overall verdict: the deck is much more honest than a typical funding pitch. It labels status, gives sources and has stop rules. Most numbers are reproducible. What can still sink it: (a) the CC-layer slides misattribute or overstate three design facts, (b) the product design already rules out continuous CPU or GPU sampling in run capture, and the deck does not mention it, (c) the prediction test cannot pass or fail with the data it plans to use, and (d) the end goal of predicting AI4Research bottlenecks is asserted, but the dominant M1 bottleneck (remote model wait) has no place in the record.
