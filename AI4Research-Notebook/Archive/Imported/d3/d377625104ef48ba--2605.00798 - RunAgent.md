# 2605.00798 · RunAgent: Interpreting Natural-Language Plans with Constraint-Guided Execution

Srivastava, Khojastepour, Chakradhar, Ulukus · University of Maryland, NEC Labs America · arXiv 1 May 2026 · 17 pp (9 body)
Read: p1-3 (motivation, agentic language, design); results from abstract

## Summary
A multi-agent platform that **executes a natural-language plan step by step** with
checking at every step. Plans are written in free prose plus a few **reserved keywords**
(IF, GOTO, FORALL, PYTHON, LLM, TOOL) that the interpreter detects deterministically,
giving plans real control flow without a rigid syntax.

## How it works (p2-3)
- At the start an LLM reasoner **derives constraints** that must hold for the plan to be
  correct, from the task and the task instance; users may add constraints and rubrics
- Each step: an LLM judge chooses execution mode (LLM reasoning, generated Python, or a
  registered tool) unless a keyword forces it; output is then validated **syntactically,
  semantically, and against the relevant subset of constraints and rubrics**, with error
  correction on failure
- **FORALL** exists because LLMs "only perform the operation on a partial subset instead
  of all items" (p2)
- Context history is filtered per step to relevant items
- Modules: Initialization & Staging (tool registry, constraint generator), Compiler,
  Executor (Fig. 1)

## Results (abstract)
Outperforms baseline LLMs and PlanGEN methods on Natural-Plan and SciBench. Specific
numbers not read; do not cite.

## Relevance
- REPORT.md §3.1-3.3: a middle point between free agent loops and AI4Research's freeze:
  the plan is fixed and **constraints are fixed at the start**, then every step is checked
  against them. Close to AI4Research's frozen EvaluationPlan idea, but the constraints are
  LLM-derived and checked partly by LLM judges
- The FORALL observation is a small, concrete reason to keep iteration in deterministic
  code, as AI4Research's scheduler does
