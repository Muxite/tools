# 2308.00352 · MetaGPT: Meta Programming for a Multi-Agent Collaborative Framework

Hong, Zhuge, Chen, Zheng, Cheng, Wang, Zhang, Wang, Yau, Lin, Zhou, Ran, Xiao, Wu, Schmidhuber · DeepWisdom, KAUST, … · ICLR 2024 · 29 pp (9 body)
Read: p1-5 (motivation, SOP design, communication protocol, executable feedback)

## Summary
Encodes human **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)** into a multi-agent software
company (Product Manager → Architect → Project Manager → Engineer → QA Engineer). The key
design claim: **handoffs between agents are structured documents** (PRDs, system
designs, interface specs, flowcharts), not free chat, which cuts the "cascading
hallucinations" of naive LLM chaining.

## How it works (p2-5)
- Role profiles (name, goal, constraints, skills/tools); agents follow ReAct-style
  behaviour
- **Structured outputs at each handoff**: "all their handovers must comply with certain
  established standards" (p2)
- **Shared message pool** with publish-subscribe: agents publish structured messages and
  subscribe by profile
- **Executable feedback**: the Engineer runs its code, and on errors consults memory,
  the PRD and the design, then debugs (+5.4% absolute on MBPP)

## Results
HumanEval **85.9%** and MBPP **87.7%** Pass@1 (then SoTA); 100% task completion on its
software-project evaluation vs AutoGPT, LangChain, AgentVerse, ChatDev (p2).

## Relevance
- REPORT.md §3.1-3.4: an early, influential statement of the idea AI4Research carries much
  further: **typed intermediate artifacts between stages** reduce error propagation.
  MetaGPT's artifacts are documents checked by other agents; AI4Research's are
  JSON-Schema-validated, hashed and gated
- The Swarm Skills paper (2605.10052) cites MetaGPT SOPs as framework-private coordination
