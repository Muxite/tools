# 2608.24306 · Who is the Agent to Blame? Localizing Faithfulness and Citation Mistakes in Agentic Deep Research

Hirsch, Wan, Wang, Stengel-Eskin, Bansal, Dagan · Bar-Ilan, UNC Chapel Hill, UT Austin · arXiv 25 Aug 2026 · 26 pp (9 body)
Read: p1-6 (method, validation, setup); results from abstract and intro. Code: github.com/eranhirs/who-is-the-agent-to-blame

## Summary
Deep-research systems pass information through several agents "like a telephone game",
and both content and citations get corrupted on the way. The paper evaluates **each
agent invocation against its own inputs**, which lets it (a) measure each agent's error
rate and (b) trace every error in the final report back to the agent that introduced it.

## How it works (p3-5)
- For each synthesised sentence, 5 LLM steps (Fig. 3): extract supporting spans from the
  agent's inputs (spans must appear **verbatim**) → none or partial = **hallucination** →
  drop uncited spans, still supported? no = **uncited input reliance** → sentence has no
  citations = **uncited output** → its citations don't cover the spans it relied on =
  **insufficient citations** → else correct
- Extracted snippets get one test: does the source support the whole snippet
  ("snippet missing context")
- Judge: gpt-5-mini with prompts from LAQuer, DEER and LongCite
- Human validation on 50 sentences: annotator κ = 0.71; method vs arbitrated labels 76%
  exact, κ = 0.62; localisation agrees on 75% (p5-6)
- Systems: Nvidia **AI-Q** and **MS-Agent** (#1 and #2 open-source on DeepResearch Bench,
  May 2026) and TrajectoryKit; 20 DeepResearch Bench tasks, ~200 sentences per agent

## Results
- "Almost every agent makes a lot of mistakes", except agents that summarise a single
  document (p1)
- Stronger-model systems shift from hallucination in shallow agents to **citation errors
  at the orchestrator**; the small-model system hallucinates everywhere (p2)
- ⭐ **84.7% of final-report errors in AI-Q originate at the orchestrator**, ~31% of them
  hallucinations, the rest citation mistakes (p1)
- 2 interventions: tell the orchestrator not to use uncited information, and **replace
  synthesised researcher notes with raw source snippets**: citation recall +5%, precision
  +3 to 7 points, no quality loss (p2)

## Limitations
- LLM-judged throughout; per-system extraction of agent inputs and outputs must be
  hand-built
- 20 tasks, 3 systems

## Relevance
- REPORT.md §3.4: evidence that errors enter at the **orchestrator**, which motivates
  keeping orchestration out of the model after planning
- ⭐ The winning intervention (**pass raw source snippets, not model summaries**) is the
  same design choice AI4Research makes by carrying verbatim, hash-bound `citation_span`s
  through the pipeline instead of paraphrases
- Its "uncited input reliance" category is what AI4Research's `unsupported_claims` list
  and verdict gate try to make impossible to hide
