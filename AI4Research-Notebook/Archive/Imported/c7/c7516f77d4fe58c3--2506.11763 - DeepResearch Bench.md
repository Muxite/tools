# 2506.11763 · DeepResearch Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Deep Research Agents

Du, Xu, Zhu, Wang, Mao · USTC, MetastoneTechnology · arXiv 13 Jun 2025 · 31 pp (9 body)
Read: body p1-9 (construction, RACE, FACT, Table 1, human consistency)

## Summary
The first dedicated benchmark for **Deep Research Agents (DRAs)**: 100 PhD-level tasks
(50 Chinese, 50 English) across 22 domains, with 2 evaluation frameworks: **RACE** for
report quality and **FACT** for citation trustworthiness.

## How it is built (p2-5)
- Topic mix taken from 96,147 real chatbot queries, filtered by DeepSeek-V3 to 44,019
  "deep research" queries and classified into 22 domains; compressed to 100 tasks written
  by PhDs or practitioners with >5 years' experience, then manually screened
- **RACE**: 4 fixed dimensions (Comprehensiveness, Insight/Depth, Instruction-Following,
  Readability); the judge LLM derives task-specific dimension weights (averaged over
  trials) and criteria, then scores the target report **relative to a reference report**
  (S_tgt / (S_tgt + S_ref)), because isolated scoring gave uniformly high scores (p5)
- **FACT**: judge LLM extracts statement-URL pairs, dedupes, fetches the page (Jina
  Reader), judges support/not-support → **Citation Accuracy** and **Effective Citations
  per task**
- Judges: Gemini-2.5-pro (RACE), Gemini-2.5-flash (FACT); reference reports come from
  Gemini-2.5-pro Deep Research (p6)

## Results (Table 1, p6)
| System | RACE overall | Citation acc. | Effective cit. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gemini-2.5-Pro Deep Research | 48.88 | 81.44 | 111.21 |
| OpenAI Deep Research | 46.98 | 77.96 | 40.79 |
| Perplexity Deep Research | 42.25 | 90.24 | 31.26 |
| Grok Deeper Search | 40.24 | 83.59 | 8.15 |
| Claude-3.7-Sonnet w/ search | 40.67 | 93.68 | 32.48 |
| Claude-3.5-Sonnet w/ search | 28.48 | 94.04 | 9.78 |

- More citations and higher accuracy trade off: the most comprehensive agents are not
  the most precise
- Human consistency: RACE pairwise agreement 71.33% vs 58.89% for a vanilla judge prompt
  (Table 2, p8); tasks with negative expert ICC were filtered before correlation

## Limitations
- Every score is an LLM judgment (Tier VIII for quality, Tier IV-by-LLM for citations)
- References come from one of the systems being evaluated (Gemini Deep Research), a
  possible bias toward its style
- Commercial systems change over time; the paper cannot confirm model versions (p6 fn.)

## Relevance
- The citation-accuracy column (77.96-94.04) is the standard external number for how
  often deep-research citations support their statements (REPORT.md §3.5, §3.7)
- FACT checks support after the fact by LLM; AI4Research checks in code that a quoted
  claim occurs in its cited section (substring match) and passes only hash-re-verified
  spans downstream, but only for verbatim quotes, and no gate fails a bad span
- ⚠ Range note: across all 16 rows citation accuracy runs 39.36-94.04; among the 4
  deep-research agents 77.96-90.24
