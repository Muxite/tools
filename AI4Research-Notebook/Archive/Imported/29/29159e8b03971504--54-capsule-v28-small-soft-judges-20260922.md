# 54: Capability Capsule schema v2.8: start extra small, block only for safety, label for quality (2026-09-22, late)

Builds on note 53. Branch `schema-v2.1-overnight` in `capsule-openjiuwen`, commit `2552f76`; not pushed.

## Why

Muk: the new library should be extra small, and CC must not be so strict that AI4Research cannot reach its goals.
The weakness found: research outputs are mostly semantic, so they can only be judged, and v2.7 let an unmeasured
judge block provisional capsules and generalists from those steps, which would have starved the library.

## The resolution

- **Invariant 14, block only for safety, label for quality.** Refused: wrong hash, protected write, undeclared effect,
  irreversible effect without approval, revoked or security-suspect capsule, failed node gate. Everything else is a
  label on each delivered claim: `judge_unmeasured`, `judge_demoted`, `exempt`, `input_unsupported`, `revoked`.
- **Judges always gate; standing sets what a pass is worth** (§4.5c): unmeasured (label), calibrated (30+ items, half
  with injected defects; supported, audited, within the step's `judge_tolerance`, 0.2 by default), measured (200+,
  under 0.05; certification). 5% of judged passes are spot-checked by a person or a reference check, so judges
  calibrate in normal sprints. Retirement still needs a measured judge.
- **Softer:** provisional capsules and generalists may bind to judged steps; `requires_certified` (off by default)
  replaces `accepts_provisional`; a sprint may set `require_supported_delivery`; a person confirms only writes,
  deletes, emissions, executions and `ext:` effects; a provisional postcondition may be judged.
- **Extra small library:** about ten seed capsules, caps of 1-3 per slot, generalists for the rest, and **import
  first** as a fifth update option when a gap recurs.
