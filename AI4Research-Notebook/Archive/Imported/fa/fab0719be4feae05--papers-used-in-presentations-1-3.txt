2605.24050 | More Skills, Worse Agents? | More Skills, Worse Agents? 2606.07904 | Contract2Tool | Contract2Tool: Learning Preconditions and Effects for Reliable Tool-Augmented LLM Agents | P1 slide 22; P2 appx | filtering a 100-tool registry by learned preconditions and effects gave 0.980 success at ~2.5k tokens, against 0.775 at ~26k when every tool is shown

2607.07436 | The Blind Curator | The Blind Curator: How a Biased Judge Silently Disables Skill Retirement in Self-Evolving Agents | P1 slide 23; P2 appx | a biased judge silently stops a curator retiring skills, while cap evictions hide it: judges must earn a standing on planted defects
2609.16313 | Cognitive Admission Control | Cognitive Admission Control: Risk-Conditioned Assurance for Consequential Actions in Agentic Distributed Systems | P1 slide 23; P2 appx | a deterministic admission controller gave 0 harmful effects in 390 trials: typed verdicts, defer on unknown, and no score

2607.02345 | SkillFuzz | SkillFuzz: Fuzzing Skill Composition for Implicit Intents Discovery in Open Skill Marketplaces | P1 slide 23; P2 appx | benign skill sets steer planners to goals nobody asked for (1,188 found): composition risk is in no single capsule
