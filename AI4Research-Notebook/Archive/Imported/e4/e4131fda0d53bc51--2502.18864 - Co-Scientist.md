# 2502.18864 · Accelerating scientific discovery with Co-Scientist (Google)

Gottweis, Weng, … Kohli, Pawlosky, Karthikesalingam, Natarajan · Google Cloud AI Research, DeepMind, Research, Stanford, Imperial · arXiv Feb 2025, revised (mentions Gemini 3) · 157 pp (25 body)
Read: body p1-14 (design, evaluation, AML validation)

## Summary
A Gemini-based multi-agent system for **hypothesis generation**, built for a
"scientist-in-the-loop" paradigm. Given a research goal in natural language it
proposes novel, testable hypotheses and experimental protocols, improving them by
spending more test-time compute on debate, tournaments and evolution. Validated in 3
biomedical settings with wet-lab experiments.

## How it works (p5-7)
- **Supervisor agent** parses the goal and allocates work to specialised agents through
  an **asynchronous task queue**
- Agents: **Generation** (incl. self-play scientific debate), **Reflection** (critique;
  with search tools), **Ranking** (pairwise **tournament**, Elo), **Evolution**
  (refine), **Proximity** (relatedness / dedupe), **Meta-review** (synthesis)
- Default hypothesis criteria: plausibility, novelty, testability, safety (p6)
- Persistent context memory for long runs; tools: web search, specialised AI models
- Scientists can steer, seed ideas and give feedback in chat

## Results
- Ablations: giving Reflection search tools "effectively prevented the hallucination of
  seemingly novel but implausible hypotheses"; debate prompts in Ranking reduced
  positional bias (p7)
- Elo of top hypotheses rises over compute across **203 research goals** (Fig. 2a);
  on 15 expert-curated goals it surpasses o1, o3-mini-high, DeepSeek R1 and the
  experts' own "best guess" **by its own Elo** (p8)
- Blinded expert evaluation on 11 goals: preference rank 2.36, novelty 3.64/5, impact
  3.09/5, best among compared systems (p11)
- Wet-lab: AML drug-repurposing candidates with selective cytotoxicity at clinically
  relevant concentrations (from 2,300 drugs × 34 cancers); liver-fibrosis epigenetic
  targets active in organoids; independently proposed an unpublished bacterial gene
  transfer mechanism (cf-PICIs) (p3, p13)

## Limitations (stated)
- "the Elo metric is auto-evaluated and not based on independent ground truth"
  (Extended Data Fig. 1, p10)
- Expert evaluations are "subjective … not objective ground truth"; small scale,
  "further studies are necessary for any reliable conclusions" (p11)
- "all experimental validations were conducted in collaboration with expert scientists,
  who provided guidance to Co-Scientist and prioritized wet-lab experiments" (p11)
