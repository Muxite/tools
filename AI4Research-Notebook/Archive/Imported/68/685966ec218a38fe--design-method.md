# Design method and architectural integrity

**Design method is integrated with project coordination.** Start at the [presentation route](project-coordination/README.md). Product reading starts at the [package overview](README.md).

The method retains product/design authority, shared-contract obligations and relevant human review. Native implementation records retain their existing authority. Tools and work-size suggestions are proposals.
