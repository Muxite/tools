# 2602.22302 · Agent Behavioral Contracts: Formal Specification and Runtime Enforcement for Reliable Autonomous AI Agents (ABC)

Bhardwaj · arXiv Feb 2026 · 71 pp (61 body incl. proofs)
Read: p1-10 (problem, contributions, related work, framework outline); results from abstract and contributions

## Summary
Brings **Design-by-Contract** (Meyer) to agents. An ABC contract C = (P, I, G, R):
**Preconditions**, **Invariants** (hard and soft), **Governance policies** (hard and soft)
and **Recovery** mechanisms, enforced at runtime over whole sessions rather than per
response. Because LLMs are non-deterministic, compliance is probabilistic: **(p, δ, k)-
satisfaction** (holds with probability ≥ p, deviation ≤ δ, recovery within k steps).

## How it works (p2-6)
- Problem framed as **behavioral drift**: agents diverge gradually over a session, e.g. a
  research agent "may start by citing verified sources but drift toward fabricated
  references as the session extends" (p2)
- Argues passive alignment is insufficient: text-level safety does not transfer to
  tool-call safety (Cartagena & Teixeira 2026), and a "self-evolution trilemma" (Wang et
  al. 2026a): continuous self-evolution, isolation from external correction, and safety
  invariance cannot coexist (p2)
- **Stochastic Drift Bound Theorem** (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck model, Lyapunov analysis):
  recovery rate γ > natural drift α bounds drift to D* = α/γ in expectation
- **Compositionality Theorem**: conditions (interface compatibility, assumption
  discharge, governance consistency, recovery independence) under which per-agent
  guarantees compose along multi-agent chains
- **ContractSpec** (YAML DSL) and **AgentAssert** runtime library, <10 ms per action
- Positions itself as complementary to Agent Contracts (2601.08815): that paper bounds
  **how much** an agent consumes, ABC bounds **how** it behaves (p4)

## Results (p3, abstract)
- AgentContract-Bench: 200 scenarios, 7 domains, 6 stress profiles; 1,980 sessions on 7
  models from 6 vendors
- Contracted agents detect **5.2-6.8 soft violations per session** that uncontracted
  baselines miss (p < 0.0001); **88-100% hard-constraint compliance**; drift bounded to
  D* < 0.27; recovery success 17-100%; reliability Θ > 0.90 on all models

## Limitations
Single author; benchmark built by the same author; contracts are hand-written; predicates
are expression checks over observable actions, not semantic truth.
