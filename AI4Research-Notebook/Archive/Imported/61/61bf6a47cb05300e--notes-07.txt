SAY: The problems, and why they hit us harder. A tool is kept because the whole run got the right answer, not because the tool worked, and a lot of research steps don't even have a right answer. Nothing tests a tool after it's rewritten. Nothing removes duplicates, so by 128 tools about half the library is near-copies. Selection is fragile: 1 threshold moves accuracy 16 points. And it builds once for a fixed benchmark and then freezes. We never stop, so the library changes while sprints are running. Their own growth study stops at 3 harvests: a 4th adds 28 tools and only 8 new capabilities, and accuracy does not move.
