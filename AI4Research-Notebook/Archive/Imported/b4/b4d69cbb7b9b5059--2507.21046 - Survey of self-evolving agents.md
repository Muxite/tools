# 2507.21046 · A Survey of Self-Evolving Agents: What, When, How, and Where to Evolve on the Path to Artificial Super Intelligence

Gao, Geng, Hua, Hu, … Ji, Wang (Mengdi) · Princeton, Tsinghua, SJTU, CMU, UIUC and others · TMLR 01/2026 · arXiv v4 16 Jan 2026 · 77 pp (52 body)
Read: p1-6 (framing, definitions), §3.3 tools (p13-14), §7 evaluation (p35-36, p46-47), §8.3 safety (p50-51). Not read end to end: §4-6 method and domain catalogue

## Summary
A taxonomy survey of agents that improve themselves after deployment. It organises the
field on 4 axes and adds an evaluation framework and a safety agenda. It collects
methods rather than measuring them, so its value to the reports is **vocabulary and
framing**, not evidence.

## The taxonomy (Fig. 3, p5)
- **What** evolves: model, context (memory, prompts), tools, architecture
  (single- or multi-agent)
- **When**: intra-test-time (within a task; ICL, SFT, RL) vs inter-test-time (between
  tasks)
- **How**: reward-based (textual feedback, internal, external rewards), imitation and
  demonstration, population-based; cross-cutting: online/offline, on/off-policy,
  reward granularity
- **Where**: general vs specialised domains (coding, GUI, finance, medical, education)
- Formal definition (p5-6): environment as a POMDP; agent system Π = (Γ, {ψ}, {C}, {W}),
  that is, architecture, models, context and tools

## Tools (§3.3, p13-14)
- 3 fronts: discovery and creation (Voyager, Alita, CREATOR, SkillWeaver, CRAFT),
  mastery by iterative refinement, and management and selection
- States that "unconstrained generation of code risks creating tools with exploitable
  vulnerabilities", and that automated verification and sandboxing are open research
  (p13)

## Evaluation (§7)
- 5 goals: **adaptivity, retention, generalisation, efficiency, safety** (Table 6);
  3 paradigms by time scale: static, short-horizon, long-horizon (Fig. 9, p36)
- ⭐ Gap (p46): "Existing tool benchmarks provide fixed APIs; **no evaluation captures
  the self-directed lifecycle of tool discovery, integration testing, and productivity
  measurement**". Written before 2604.00392, which partly fills it
- Fair comparison is "infeasible at present": reporting, pipelines, backbones and
  architectures all differ; Table 11 is "an illustrative snapshot" (p46-47)

## Safety (§8.3, p50-51)
- Emergent risks: behaviour drift ("misevolution"), reward hacking in memory evolution,
  unsafe self-created or ingested tools
- Prescribed guardrails: **sandbox every generated tool, with static analysis and
  vulnerability scanning before integration**; **audit trails with rollback for every
  self-modification**; continuous monitoring and red-teaming for long-horizon drift

## Limitations
- Catalogue, not measurement; many cited systems are preprints
- ⚠ The ASI framing in the title and Fig. 1 is aspirational; the Darwin epigraph is
  flagged as a misattribution by the authors themselves (p2)

## Relevance
- **Capsule report**: supplies the terms (what/when/how/where; the 5 evaluation goals)
  and a published statement that tool-lifecycle evaluation is missing. Its guardrail
  list (sandbox, pre-integration verification, audit trail, rollback) is the checklist
  a capsule admission process answers
- **General report**: a framing citation for §1 and §6. AI4Research is not a
  self-evolving agent in this sense: its pipeline is fixed per run and only its
  capsule library grows. The survey is cited for vocabulary, not for comparison
