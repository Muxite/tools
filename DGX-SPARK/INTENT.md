- Optimize useful verified decisions per time/resources, not capsule count, branch count, passing-test count or an isolated maximum tokens/s value.

Follow that current direction: preserve llama.cpp as a diagnostic/recovery control; do not switch the immediate baseline and lose attribution.

Resolve actual source, dirty-tree bytes, binary, image, pack and effective configuration before using any result.

Jetson findings are historical mechanism/tooling evidence. They do not qualify Spark, its CUDA architecture, numerical behavior or memory accounting. Older Muse plans are background; the inspected current campaign uses the pinned DeepSeek pack. Old deadlines and budgets are never renewed by inclusion here.
